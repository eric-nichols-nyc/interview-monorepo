## Structure Preferences
### Opening
- [ ] Start with a strong hook/connection to the company
- [ ] Begin with a personal anecdote
- [ ] Lead with your strongest qualification
- [ ] Reference a specific company achievement/news

### Body Paragraphs
- [ ] Use specific examples and metrics
- [ ] Tell stories that demonstrate skills
- [ ] Connect experience directly to job requirements
- [ ] Show knowledge of company/industry

### Closing
- [ ] Express genuine enthusiasm
- [ ] Include a call to action
- [ ] Reference next steps
- [ ] Keep it confident but not presumptuous
